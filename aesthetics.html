Aesthetics of Romance · Colors of the Soul

The ancients of China condensed the mountains, rivers, sun, moon, and seasons into stunning romantic Chinese color palettes. Flowing through millennia, classical poetry remains the ultimate annotation to these colors. Let us look back across five thousand years in poetry and painting, rediscovering the profound implications of Eastern aesthetics.
