Guqin: The Ritual Instrument Connecting Heaven and Earth

The Guqin is not merely a musical instrument, but a sacred legacy from our ancestors connecting us to the cosmos. Through pure physical resonance and unique rhythms, it harmonizes with the human viscera and brainwaves, achieving inner and outer calibration.

Frequency Down-Regulation

Modern minds often linger in anxious beta waves. The deep frequencies of the Guqin forcefully guide brainwaves from agitated states down to deep-healing alpha or theta waves.

Five Tones Healing Organs

Each of the five viscera possesses its intrinsic frequency. The 'Gong, Shang, Jiao, Zhi, Yu' tones act as precise biological keys, aligning disordered qi through pure resonance.

Biological Respiration

Techniques like Yin, Rou, Chuo, and Zhu create continuous fluctuating curves, perfectly mirroring human respiration and heartbeat rhythms to awaken deep cellular responses.
